Team

Clarisse Bardiot

www.clarissebardiot.info

Clarisse Bardiot, STAGE project PI, is Professor of history of contemporary theatre and digital humanities at Rennes 2 University. Her research focuses on performing arts digital traces, creative processes analysis, the history and aesthetics of digital performance, the preservation of digital works, and experimental publishing. She is the author of Performing Arts and Digital Humanities. From Traces to Data (Wiley / Iste, 2021).

Jacob Hart

jacob-hart.com

Jacob Hart is currently a postdoctoral researcher on the Stage Project at Université Rennes 2, France. He obtained his PhD in musicology at the University of Huddersfield (UK) in 2021 where I was a member of the ERC-funded FluCoMa Project (Fluid Corpus Manipulation). His research centres around tracking the creative process of techno-fluent composers and developing new approaches to computational musicology.

Erell Choulette

Erell Choulette is a computer engineer and UX designer. A double graduate of the University of Technology of Compiègne, her academic background combining artificial intelligence, cognitive sciences, and philosophy has been enriched by international experiences. She joined the team after working at IRISA on the Triangle project.
