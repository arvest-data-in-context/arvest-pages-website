The Arvest ecosystem

Arvest is the main environment: online, nothing to install, and made to be easy for anyone, students, researchers, archivists, artists. Around it are tools for more specialised work. The browser extension brings the web in. mmm and the LA-PA editor run on your own computer, for corpora that stay with you and for expert work: reading whole archives with language models, writing sourced, structured history. They all speak IIIF and talk to Arvest.

Where documents are gathered, compared, annotated, linked and shared: images, video, sound, texts and PDFs, all as IIIF. An account is all you need, and everything the other tools make ends up here.
